Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The PlotBench desktop app

PlotBench does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. It has no accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising and no automatic update check, and it makes no network connections of its own.

The app reads and writes only the files you choose to open or save, and it keeps its settings and templates on your computer (in your user profile's application-data folder). None of this leaves your device. The same applies to every edition: Microsoft Store, Windows installer, macOS and Linux.

Links in the app (for example to this website or the source code) open in your own web browser. What happens there is covered by that website's policy.

This website

plotbench.vercel.app is hosted by Vercel and uses Google Analytics to count visits. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects anonymous usage data such as the pages viewed, the referring site, your approximate country and your browser type. It does not tell us who you are. See Google's privacy policy. You can block it with any content blocker, or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Vercel keeps standard server logs for security and operation.

Children

PlotBench collects no data from anyone, including children.
